Sack Math

The 283 lemons at 7 lemons a sack take 41 sacks, the last sack holding 3 lemons.

Boundary: arithmetic only Sack Math counts an event's grove lemons with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the sacks the lemons take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a lemon, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your lemons, your sacks or your venue and does not know whether the lemons will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a sack is worth its price or a lemon is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 283 lemons, 7 lemons a sack, 20 lemons a week, $6.50 a sack and 4 lemons a minute.

The weeks truth

The 283 lemons at 20 lemons a week last 14 full weeks with 3 lemons left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 41 sacks at $6.50 a sack come to $266.50, $0.94 a lemon.

The time truth

The 283 lemons at 4 lemons a minute take 71 minutes, 1 hour 11 minutes.
